FEATURE 1 - MINIMAP (top-right corner)
- Circular or rounded-square minimap fixed in the top-right corner of the HUD. Move the
  performance overlay below it.
- Draw it on a 2D canvas from the cached base map in MapView.tsx. Do not add a second
  3D camera or render pass for it.
- Show: player arrow with heading, explored/unexplored fog, discovered landmarks,
  the waypoint (clamped to the rim with a direction marker when off-map), north marker.
- Key N cycles three zoom levels (near, medium, far) and shows the current level briefly.
- Two modes: rotating (player-up) and north-up. The settings screen has on/off, mode
  and size.
- Redraw at most 10 times per second via useTick; never per frame.
- Hidden while a lens tool is active (camera, video, binoculars, macro), while any
  overlay is open, and when the HUD is hidden with H.
- M still opens the full map.

FEATURE 2 - VIEW SYSTEM
- Keep V as the toggle and keep viewMode in the store. Add an over-the-shoulder
  third-person camera with terrain and structure collision so it never clips through
  the ground, trunks or walls, and a smooth 0.25 s transition between views.
- Lens tools still force first person. On leaving the tool, return to the view the
  player had before.
- Scanner, map, journal and bare hands must work in both views.
- Persist the chosen view in settings.

FEATURE 3 - HAND-HELD RESEARCH TOOLS
- Build low-poly procedural props with GeoBuilder, matching the existing art style.
  No external model files. Props: binoculars, camera, bio scanner (with a lit screen),
  macro lens, folded paper map, field journal, specimen tray/vial.
- Mapping: binoculars/camera/video/macro show the prop being raised to the eye for
  about 0.2 s, then the existing lens view takes over, and the prop lowers again on exit.
  Scanner stays in hand while scanning. Opening the map (M) or journal (J) raises the
  map or journal before the panel opens. Inspecting a plant, rock, track or artifact
  with E shows the specimen tray.
- First person: render props as a view model attached to the camera, in a separate
  pass after clearing depth, with its own fixed field of view, so props never clip into
  the world and are not affected by zoom. Add idle sway, walk bob and a short
  equip/unequip animation; no sway when the player stands still for precise aiming.
- Third person: attach the same prop to the avatar's hand with a matching arm pose.
- The view model must never appear in photographs, recorded video, the depth-of-field
  pass or the photo-score calculation. Prove this with a test.
- Low preset: simplified props, no extra lights. Setting to turn held items off.

FEATURE 4 - HUD BADGES
- Replace the loose chips with one badge component and a consistent layout:
  bottom-left: tool bar showing each tool with its key, the active one highlighted;
  bottom-right: time of day, weather, and a collection badge (species found / 36,
  with a small dot when something new was collected since Quick View was last opened);
  under the minimap: biome name and map coordinates;
  top-left: keep the mission tracker, add a compact badge for earned mission badges;
  contextual badges: recording, slow motion, swimming/underwater, crouching, sprinting,
  waypoint distance.
- Badges use the existing fonts by role: Chakra Petch (--display) for labels,
  JetBrains Mono (--mono) for numbers with tabular figures, Be Vietnam Pro (--sans) for text.
- Glass style matching the current HUD. Nothing may cover the centre of the screen,
  the compass or the interaction prompt. Must not overflow at 1280x720 or 960x540,
  in Vietnamese or English.

FEATURE 5 - QUICK VIEW OF COLLECTED ITEMS
- Hold Q to peek at a Quick View panel; release to close. It must not pause the game,
  release the pointer lock or need the mouse. The player can keep walking while it is open.
- Place it on the right side under the minimap, sliding in within 0.15 s. It must not
  cover the centre of the screen.
- One row per collection, each with an icon, a count and the most recent items:
  species discovered (found / 36, last five portraits with names),
  photographs (count, best score, last three thumbnails),
  videos (count, last recorded subject),
  artifacts, field notes, landmarks found, and mission badges earned (earned / 5).
- Mark items collected since the panel was last opened as new, and clear the mark when
  the panel closes. Keep this marker in session state only; do not change the save format.
- Read everything from the existing store. Reuse stored thumbnails and species
  portraits; never load full-size photos or video for this panel. Cache object URLs and
  revoke them when the panel unmounts.
- Empty collections show a short hint on how to earn the first item, not a blank row.
- The panel footer shows "J: open journal". The inventory (I) and journal (J) keep
  working as before; Quick View is a glance, not a replacement.
- Disabled while a lens tool is active or any overlay is open.
